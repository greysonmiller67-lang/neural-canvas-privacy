Privacy Policy — NeuralCanvas

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026
App: NeuralCanvas · com.proApps.aiimagegenerator
Publisher: greybits · Contact: greysonmiller67@gmail.com

This policy explains what the app collects, what it sends, and what it never touches. It is written in plain language. If anything is unclear, write to the address above.

1. What the app does

NeuralCanvas turns text prompts into AI-generated images. It runs on your phone, uses Pollinations.ai to render each image, and shows ads from Google AdMob so it can stay free. There is no account system, no sign-up and no user profile.

2. What stays on your device

Stored in private app storage that only this app can read:

  • Generated images and the prompts that produced them, so they appear in the Recent strip
  • Your preferences — default style, aspect ratio, image quality, sound, haptics, animated background

None of it is transmitted anywhere on its own; the only exception is a report you choose to send (section 9). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of it permanently. You can also clear it any time from Settings → Storage → Clear cache.

If you tap Download, a copy is written to your phone's gallery (Pictures/NeuralCanvas). If you tap Share, the image is handed to whichever app you choose through Android's standard share sheet, and that app then handles it under its own policy. Files copied to the gallery or sent to other apps remain on your phone, or with those apps, after NeuralCanvas is uninstalled.

3. What is sent when you generate an image

When you tap Generate, the app sends over HTTPS to Pollinations.ai:

  • the prompt text you typed, including any style preset wording
  • the image dimensions derived from your aspect-ratio and quality choices
  • a randomly generated seed number
  • an instruction to apply Pollinations.ai's safety filter

The app authenticates with a developer API key that it holds on your behalf. You do not need a Pollinations account and are never asked for credentials. Pollinations.ai may log requests to operate the service and prevent abuse. See pollinations.ai.

Nothing identifying you or your device is attached — no account, no user ID, no advertising ID.

Do not type personal, confidential or sensitive information into the prompt box. Treat anything you enter as text you are sending to an outside AI service.

3.1 Content safety

NeuralCanvas does not create nude, sexual or graphically violent images. Before anything is sent, the app checks your prompt on your device and refuses those requests; that check sends nothing anywhere. Every request that does go out also asks Pollinations.ai to apply its own safety filter, which refuses sexual and violent prompts before any image is made.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner, interstitial and app-open ads supplied by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier you control in Android settings)
  • IP address, used for approximate location, fraud prevention and frequency capping
  • coarse location derived from IP — the app requests no location permission and has no access to GPS
  • device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, time spent)
  • diagnostic data from the ad SDK

We do not receive your Advertising ID or IP address ourselves; Google processes this data to serve the ads. See AdMob & AdSense and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4.1 Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in a region covered by the GDPR or the UK GDPR, the app shows a consent message from Google's certified Consent Management Platform the first time it opens, before any ad is requested. Your answer determines whether ads are personalised, and no ad request is made until you have answered — the banner does not even load.

You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app. That entry point appears only for users who were shown a message. Outside those regions no message is shown and the app does not ask.

5. Voice input (optional)

If you tap the microphone icon on the Create screen, the app launches your device's built-in speech recognition, typically provided by Google. Your voice is processed by that system under Google's privacy policy. NeuralCanvas itself never records, stores or transmits audio — it receives only the transcribed text, which then becomes your prompt.

7. Children's privacy

NeuralCanvas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through this app, contact us at the address above and we will assist where we can.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The dates at the top reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

9. Reporting an image

AI models can occasionally produce output that is offensive, unsafe or otherwise wrong. Every generated image can be reported from inside the app — tap the flag at the top of the Result screen, or Report this image below the refine box. You pick a reason and can add a note. The report is sent without leaving the app, and the image is removed from the app straight away.

A report contains the reason, your note if you wrote one, the prompt, the image's reference number, style, aspect ratio and creation time, and the app version. It does not contain the image itself, and nothing identifying you or your device.

Reports are submitted to a Google Form operated by greybits; Google stores the responses for us in a spreadsheet only we can read, under the Google Privacy Policy. If you are offline, the report waits on your device and is sent the next time the app starts. Reports are read by a person and used to improve the app's content filtering.
